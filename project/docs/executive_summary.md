# Executive Summary

## Purpose
The Care Transition Efficiency & Placement Outcome Analytics project reframes aggregate UAC operational reporting around movement through the care pipeline.

## Dataset
The supplied CSV contains 1,170 source rows. 450 rows lacked valid dates and were excluded, leaving 720 valid dated observations covering 2023-01-12 to 2025-12-21.

## Headline indicators
- Reported intake: 67,337
- Reported transfers: 92,641
- Reported discharges: 124,853
- Weighted transfer efficiency: 75.03%
- Weighted discharge effectiveness: 2.86%
- Aggregate throughput: 185.42%
- Maximum combined active load: 11,762

## Operational signals
The largest report-to-report increase in combined active load was 730, while the largest decrease was -922. The project-defined bottleneck framework classified 180 observations as High or Critical, including 72 Critical observations.

## Interpretation
These signals identify periods for review. They do not establish why a period occurred or imply that every stage represents the same unique children. Aggregate throughput can exceed 100% because entries and exits may represent different cohorts and periods.

## Recommended use
Use the dashboard as a monitoring and triage tool:
- inspect sustained active-load growth;
- compare transfers and discharges with inflow;
- review low-efficiency periods;
- use monthly and rolling trends to distinguish persistent from isolated pressure;
- investigate flagged periods using operational context not present in the dataset.

## Future scope
A case-level dataset could support actual stage duration, time-to-placement, geographic comparisons, case complexity, and more rigorous predictive or causal analysis.
